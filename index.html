Practical Emergency Plumbing Advice

What to Do During a Plumbing Emergency

A few sensible precautions may help reduce damage while you seek professional assistance.

1. Stop the Water If Safe

For a leaking fixture, look for the nearby shutoff valve and turn it clockwise. For a major leak, locate your home's main water shutoff and close it if you can safely reach it. Don't force a stuck valve.

2. Keep Clear of Hazards

Stay away from standing water near electrical outlets, appliances or wiring. Avoid using sinks or toilets affected by a sewage backup. If you smell gas, leave the building immediately and contact your gas utility or emergency services from a safe location.

3. Describe the Problem Clearly

When enquiring about plumbing help, explain the leak or blockage, whether water is still flowing, and your location. Ask the independent provider about availability, likely response times, pricing and qualifications before arranging work.

This is general safety information, not a substitute for professional assessment. No particular plumber, arrival time or 24-hour availability is guaranteed.
