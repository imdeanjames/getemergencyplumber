Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit getemergencyplumber.com or telephone the number shown on this independent plumbing information and referral website.

1. Introduction

Get Emergency Plumber (https://getemergencyplumber.com/) is an independently operated informational and lead-generation website. We provide information about plumbing-related services and offer a way to enquire about being connected with independent service providers. We do not perform plumbing work ourselves.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, and shared when you browse this website or make a telephone enquiry.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website and phone service, information may include:

  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us, such as your name, location, and details about a plumbing problem.
  • Your telephone number and caller-identification or call-routing information, where available through the telecommunications service.
  • Details you choose to share during a telephone enquiry, such as the type of service you need and your preferred contact arrangements.
  • Limited technical information associated with website visits, such as your IP address, browser type, or server access logs, to the extent collected by website hosting infrastructure.

The current website does not include a contact form or a dedicated website analytics or advertising-tracking script. We will update this policy if those features are introduced.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, assist with connecting callers to relevant independent providers, facilitate requested consultations or services, maintain and improve the website and quality of enquiries, help prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal requirements.

4. Lead Generation and Third-Party Service Providers

This website operates as an independent plumbing information and lead-generation service. Telephone enquiries may be answered, handled, transferred or referred, including through third-party telecommunications and referral services, to independent businesses offering relevant plumbing services.

The website operator may receive compensation for qualifying telephone enquiries or referrals. Information relevant to your enquiry, including contact details and details of the service requested, may be shared with the organisations involved in routing and responding to the enquiry.

Independent providers are responsible for their own work, pricing, availability, appointments and business practices. Calling this website does not, by itself, authorise unrelated marketing calls or messages.

5. Call Tracking and Recording

Telephone enquiries may be tracked, routed and handled using third-party telecommunications services. Information processed may include caller identification, the telephone number, call date and time, duration, routing information and call outcome.

Incoming calls and voicemail messages may be recorded and reviewed to assess enquiries, maintain service quality, prevent misuse and assist with call handling or referrals. Recordings and relevant enquiry information may be processed or shared with telecommunications suppliers and, where appropriate, businesses involved in responding to the enquiry.

Calls may also be transferred to independent service providers, whose own call-handling and recording practices may apply. Not every call will necessarily be recorded. Where notice or consent is required for recording, the relevant party must provide it and obtain consent as required by applicable law. Please do not include payment card information or other sensitive details in voicemail messages.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

The current page does not intentionally set cookies for advertising or run a dedicated analytics script. Essential website infrastructure or hosting providers may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site. If cookies, analytics, or advertising tools are added later, this policy will be updated to describe them as appropriate.

7. Information Sharing

Information connected with an enquiry, including call details and recordings where applicable, may be shared with independent businesses responding to enquiries and with telecommunications, call-routing, referral, hosting and other technical service providers involved in operating the website and telephone service. Information may also be disclosed where required by law. These parties may have their own privacy practices and responsibilities.

8. Data Security and Retention

We aim to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled and the services used. No website, telephone network, or data transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

Information is retained only as needed for legitimate operational, referral, dispute-resolution, or legal purposes, subject to the practices of relevant service providers. No specific retention period is promised here.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. To make a privacy enquiry, contact the website operator at dean AT djfpublishing DOT com (replace AT with @ and DOT with a full stop). We may need to verify your request and may not control records held independently by a referred service provider.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Independent plumbing providers, telecommunications services, and external websites may maintain their own privacy notices and practices. Review their information where relevant, particularly before providing additional personal details or agreeing to services.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website's services, information-handling practices or legal requirements change materially. A revised version will be published here with an updated date where appropriate. The call-handling and referral activities described above may be used without changing this policy solely because the routing destination changes.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or information handled by this independent website, email dean AT djfpublishing DOT com (replace AT with @ and DOT with a full stop).

This website is independently operated and is not the former plumbing business. It provides plumbing service information and facilitates telephone enquiries; it does not perform plumbing work itself.
